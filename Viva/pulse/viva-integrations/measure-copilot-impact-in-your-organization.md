# Measure Copilot impact in your organization

The Microsoft Copilot Dashboard, powered by Viva Insights, helps organizations maximize the value of Copilot for M365 by providing actionable insights to help organizations get ready to deploy AI, drive adoption based on how AI is transforming workplace behavior and measure the impact of Copilot.

Viva Pulse integrates into the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard to provide a way for organizations to capture sentiment data that helps leaders measure the effectiveness and usage of Copilot. With a research-backed template, Viva Pulse enables leaders and IT admins to send their own surveys to capture feedback quickly from their teams and requires minimal admin setup to get started. Leaders and IT admins with access to the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard can launch Pulse surveys directly to gain insights into how Copilot impacts their workforce.

There are two ways for leaders and IT admins to capture sentiment about Copilot from their teams, either by (1) launching a Copilot impact Pulse survey from the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard or (2) launching a Pulse survey from within Viva Pulse.

To integrate Pulse surveys results into the Copilot Dashboard, ensure you have a license to Viva Suite or Viva Workplace Analytics and Employee Feedback.

### Capture sentiment about Copilot from the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard

Leaders and IT admins with access to the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard can launch a Pulse survey to capture feedback from their teams and this feedback will be mapped to workplace patterns data collected by Viva Insights.

1. Access the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard, powered by Viva Insights. For further information, read the documentation [here](/viva/insights/org-team-insights/copilot-dashboard).
2. Select the **Sentiment** tab in the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the page and see the **Employee sentiment about Copilot** section and select the **Start new Pulse survey** button.

### Capture sentiment about Copilot from Viva Pulse

If leaders and IT admins do not have access to the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard, they can also launch a Pulse survey within Viva Pulse to capture feedback on Copilot impact across their teams.

1. Access the Copilot impact template in Viva Pulse via the web [here](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2259628).
2. Access the Copilot impact template in Viva Pulse via the Teams app by visiting the homepage and selecting **Browse all templates** in the **Get started by asking for feedback on…** section at the top of the homepage. Scroll down to the **For managers and leaders** section and click on **M365 Copilot impact**.

### Customize the Copilot impact template in Viva Pulse

The Copilot impact questions are all read-only to preserve accurate relationships between sentiment data collected from Viva Pulse and workplace patterns data collected by Viva Insights and the four core questions in the template cannot be deleted. To customize the Copilot impact template in Viva Pulse, you can:

1. Add questions from the **Microsoft Copilot impact topic** in the question library.
2. Delete questions added in from the Microsoft Copilot impact topic in the question library.
3. Reorder questions in the template with the up and down arrows on each question.
4. Click **Next** to finalize your Pulse and add or remove survey recipients as needed.
5. Update the survey end date and time as needed. The default length of a Pulse is 7 days.
6. Add an optional personal note that will be shown to survey recipients when they receive a notification about your Pulse.
7. Click **Next** to send the Copilot impact Pulse.

### Review the reports in the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard and in Viva Pulse

If the leader or IT admin created the Pulse from the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard, when the Pulse closes, the notification to review the results will have two links: one link to the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard to review the sentiment data collected by Viva Pulse mapped to the workplace patterns data collected by Viva Insights and one link to the Pulse report to view the sentiment data in more detail.

If the leader or IT admin did not create the Pulse from the Microsoft Copilot Dashboard, then their Pulse closed notification will only have one link to the Pulse report to review the sentiment data in more detail.
